Community Built

"Community Built" units are designed and contributed by i-sparkle community members. Want to publicly share your custom unit? Select the Publish option in the unit details pop-up. Or open your unit in the Authoring Tool, go to Unit Info, and tap Publish!

*Please note: While i-sparkle staff have reviewed these units, we cannot specifically vouch for their effectiveness or quality. Please examine the units carefully before using in your classrooms. Thanks!
